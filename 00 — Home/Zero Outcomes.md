## Cross-outcome foundation

Maintain it as a shared foundation, with explicit links from each blueprint to the data quality it requires. Avoid assuming every use case needs the same CMDB completeness.

## What each outcome must define

- **Business problem:** whose problem we are solving and why it matters.
    
- **Target result:** the observable improvement we seek.
    
- **Measures:** baseline, metric definitions, targets, and observation period.
    
- **Blueprints:** the bounded use cases that advance the outcome.
    
- **Ownership:** accountable outcome and blueprint owners.
    
- **Evidence:** validated results, limitations, and readiness for reuse.
    

## From outcome to implementation

1. Select the outcome and a specific customer use case.
    
2. Choose the relevant blueprint and agree its scope.
    
3. Assess prerequisites and customer decisions.
    
4. Follow the applicable implementation guide.
    
5. Validate readiness gates and measure results.
    
6. Refine the approach before extending it to more services.
    

## Portfolio review

Review each blueprint against these questions:

- Is the scope clear enough to implement?
    
- Are the procedures supported by applicable documentation?
    
- Which decisions or evidence gaps remain?
    
- Has it been tested, and within what environment and scope?
    
- Are the results measurable and repeatable?
    
- Who owns the next action?
